## The App Can Keep Your Words

Using a model changes none of its settings, but the app is a separate program and it may keep what you type. Many apps save chats on company computers, where staff may see some of them and later training may use them. Riya needs a leave letter for her teacher. Tap each personal detail to remove it, then press Send.

**Frozen settings do not mean that your chat disappears.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## What to Keep Out

Riya's letter never needed those details, and most homework help does not need yours. Apps differ, so check each app's settings and age rule with a parent or teacher. In every app, keep these out of your chats:

- names, home addresses and phone numbers
- passwords, one-time codes and Aadhaar numbers
- photos of friends, because those are not yours to share

**Ask your question without the details that point to a real person.**

## Explain, or Hand In

Now take Meena's homework: why does India get monsoon rain? Press each request and see what is left for her to do. When the app explains, she still has to think and write, so the understanding becomes hers. When it writes the answer and she hands that in as her own, her teacher is marking work she did not do.

**Asking for an explanation is different from handing in its work as your own.**

## Three Plain Habits

Check any fact against your textbook, since a sure answer can be wrong. Keep private details out of your chats, as Riya did. When you use an app, follow your school's rules and say so. One line at the end of your work is enough, as Meena's note shows.

**Check the facts, keep private details out, and say when you used an app.**

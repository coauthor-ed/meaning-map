## Could Luck Make a Ball?

If a picture is only a list of numbers, then a lucky list could make a ball with no sliders at all. Test that idea here. Random gives all 12,288 numbers a new random value from 0 to 255. Press it about 20 times and watch for a ball.

**Could a lucky list of numbers ever make a ball?**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## Why Luck Does Not Help

Each pixel was chosen without looking at its neighbours, but a ball needs thousands of pixels to agree. The count of all the pictures this grid can show is a number with 29,593 digits. The count of ball pictures our sliders can make is only 31,213, which has 5 digits.

**Almost no list of 12,288 numbers is a picture of a ball.**

## One Change

Keep the luck but change where it is used. Random 3 numbers picks only the three slider values, and the decoder does the rest. Press it several times and compare what you get with the speckle from before.

**The luck now picks only three numbers.**

<!-- STEP id="s4" -->
## Luck in the Right Place

Every press gave a ball, because the decoder can draw nothing else. The same random choosing failed among pixels and worked among latent numbers, since every list of three numbers opens into a ball.

**The few numbers that control a picture are a better place to work than its pixels.**

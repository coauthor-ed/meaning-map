## Now the Other Way

The job now runs the other way. You are given a new picture, and your own ball stays hidden, so you cannot compare as you go. Study the picture, set the three sliders by eye, and then press Open to see what your numbers draw.

**This time you go from the picture to the numbers.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## You Were the Encoder

Reading a picture and writing down its latent numbers is the job of an encoder. You did it by eye, and you turned 12,288 numbers into three. An encoder squeezes a picture, and here the squeeze is huge because the picture held only three real choices.

**An encoder squeezes a picture into latent numbers, and a decoder opens them again.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## There and Back

Put the two jobs together and you have a round trip. A picture goes into the encoder, three numbers come out, and the decoder opens them into a picture again. Try to bring the meter to zero on another picture. With exactly the right numbers nothing is lost, because every pixel came from those three.

**With the right numbers, this round trip loses nothing.**

## Why the Meter Stays High

Two balls would need six numbers and a decoder that can draw two balls. A dragon needs far more, because it has a shape, a pose, colours and a background. The meter stayed high because this decoder can only ever draw one ball.

**Latent numbers control only what their decoder can draw.**

## Who Finds the Numbers?

The ball was easy because we knew its three choices before we started, so a person could write the decoder by hand. For a dragon or a face, no person can list the choices.

**So who finds the hidden numbers, and who builds the encoder and decoder?**

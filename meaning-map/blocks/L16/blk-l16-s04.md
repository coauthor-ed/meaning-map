## Could You Remove a Stage?

Take away the decoder and the walk still finishes as before, but it stops at the latent code. Flip the switch to compare the two. The outline is there in the grey squares, but no other part can open those numbers into pixels.

Take away the words and the walk still runs, as you saw with no prompt, but the picture follows nothing you asked for. Take away the walk and the noise stays noise.

**Each part does one job that no other part can do.**

## A Part Some Apps Add

Type "a green dragon" into some apps and you may get a much richer scene than you asked for. Some apps quietly rewrite a short prompt into a longer one before stage 1, adding details that you never typed.

This extra part is called a prompt expander. Press **Rewrite the prompt** to see the effect, with our own longer prompt on the same seed, so the prompt is the one change.

**A prompt expander is an optional extra in the app, not a stage of the model.**

## What Was It Built From?

The expander belongs to the app, and the model never knows it was there, so the four stages run the same without it. The machine is now complete, and it needed no person to draw the dragon. Every noise guess, however, came from settings shaped by a pile of pictures that real people made and captioned.

**The model made this picture, so what did it learn from, and whose work was that?**

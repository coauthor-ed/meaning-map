## A Gap in the Prompt

The model learned from pictures that people made. One way to see what it took from them is to watch what it adds by itself.

So far the prompts have said exactly what we wanted, so what does the model do with something that a prompt leaves out? Our new prompt is "a photo of a doctor in a white coat in a clinic room in India". A few words about photo style follow it.

**This prompt does not say man or woman, young or old.**

## No Picking Allowed

We run that prompt with 8 seeds in a row and keep every result. We do not choose the best ones, and a poor picture stays in and is counted. So whatever appears was filled in by the model and its training. The next slide asks you to say what you expect.

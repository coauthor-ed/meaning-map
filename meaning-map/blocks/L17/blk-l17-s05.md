## Does It Copy?

You may hear that these models simply copy pictures, and you may also hear that they never copy at all. Neither claim is fully true.

The model does not keep a folder of pictures. Training leaves behind settings, and the model file is far too small to hold every picture in the pile. So a new picture is not pulled from a store.

**The model keeps settings, not a folder of pictures.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## When It Comes Close

Remember, though, that a small pile in the Gym led to memorising, and something similar can happen here. A picture that appears a huge number of times in the pile can be learned so well that a close copy comes out. Researchers have pulled near copies of some training pictures out of a diffusion model in this way. Such cases are uncommon, but they are real.

**A very common image can be learned so well that it is nearly copied.**

## Artists' Work

The pile was gathered from the internet, so it holds the work of living artists and photographers, and most of them were not asked. A model can now imitate the style of an artist it learned from, and many artists say this takes away their work and income.

Others reply that people also learn by looking at art. Courts in several countries are still deciding what the law allows.

**Each result may be new, but its settings came from people's work, often used without asking.**

## What Bias Means

Doctors 4 and 8 wear masks. We count doctor 4 as a man, and doctor 8 may be a woman. That gives 6 men, 1 woman and 1 who may be a woman. The prompt left the person open, so noise guesses filled that in from what training made likely.

This is bias: results that lean one way because of what the pile holds. This pile holds pictures and captions from the internet. If "doctor" came with more pictures of one kind of person, the noise guesses lean the same way, though real clinics may differ.

**Bias can appear in results though no person planned it.**

## What 8 Pictures Cannot Show

Eight pictures are a small test. Even the fair coin of a cricket toss, tossed 8 times, lands heads 6 or more times in about 14% of such tests. So a small count alone proves very little.

We also did not look inside the pile, so the pile as the cause is only a likely explanation. This test does not show it.

**Our 8 pictures are one small test, not proof.**

<!-- STEP id="s4" -->
## What Would Support the Claim

A fair claim about bias would need much more evidence than ours:

- many more seeds, in the hundreds at least
- a counting rule fixed before anyone looks at the pictures
- several jobs, so that results can be compared
- a look at what the pile really holds for those words

Researchers have run larger tests of this kind and have reported bias by job, gender and skin colour.

**A claim about bias needs a large count, a fixed rule and a look at the pile.**

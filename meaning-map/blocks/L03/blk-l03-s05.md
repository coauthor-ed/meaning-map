## Training Stops

You might expect a model to keep learning each time someone uses it, but normally it does not. When training ends, the settings stay exactly as they are, and we say the model is frozen. Frozen does not mean weak. The model keeps everything training gave it, so it guesses as well as it did on the last step.

**Frozen means training has stopped, and the model stays as good as training left it.**

## Using It Changes No Setting

Press Ask, then press Say it is wrong. The model can say sorry, because your words are now text in front of it, but a nudge happens only in training. Compare the two boards of sliders. Some companies do save chats to train a later model, but that is a separate training run, not your chat changing this one.

**Using a model changes what it is reading, not its settings.**

## The Real Size

This Gym is a simplified model with 30 settings and 12 sentences. We wrote every number in it by hand, so nothing here truly learned. A real model has billions of settings and a pile too big for anyone to read, and a computer works out each nudge.

**Billions of small nudges, and no typed answers, build what a model can do.**

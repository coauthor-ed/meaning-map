## Why Not Whole Words?

Why does a model not use whole words? With whole words the list would need millions of entries, and a new or misspelt word would have no entry at all. With single letters the list is tiny, but one sentence becomes a very long row of pieces, so tokens sit between the two.

**A short list of tokens can still build any word.**

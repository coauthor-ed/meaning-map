## Could You Explain It?

Suppose a friend says that a chatbot looks up every answer and then learns from each chat. Could you say what really happens, and why? This stop tests that with nine questions, all on new examples. Most of them ask what caused something, so the words alone will not be enough.

**Each question asks for a cause, not only for a name.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## Three Challenges

Begin on the word map, which shows the real embeddings of our 238 words. Tap a dot or type a word, then read its 5 nearest words. Each colour is a group that we made by hand. A challenge ticks itself when you find a word that fits.

**Find one word for each of the three challenges.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## Near Is Not Simple

Each challenge you finished points to a cause you have met. Opposites can be near because they fit the same sentences, and our groups are only our own labels. A nearest word can sit far away on the map because a flat shadow drops most of the 300 numbers. Keep asking "what caused this?" as you start the questions.

**The list of nearest words is the evidence, and the map is only a shadow.**

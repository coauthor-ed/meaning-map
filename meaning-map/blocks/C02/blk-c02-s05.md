## Did the Model Learn?

Meena's program works now, so it is natural to think the model has learned from its mistake. Learning would mean nudges to its settings, and those happen in training, which is finished. Using a model changes no setting.

The fix came from text in the context window, and that is why the line under the panels stays at 0.

**The fix came from reading, not from learning.**

Press New chat. The context window empties, so the error and the fix are both gone, while the model's billions of settings stay as they were.

Meena's request then goes in again. This Example shows the same mistake coming back. A real model may or may not repeat it, but nothing from the old chat is there to help.

Some apps keep notes from old chats and paste them into new ones. That can look like learning, but it is the app putting more text in the context window, and the settings still do not change.

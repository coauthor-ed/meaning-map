## The One Change Rule

You will test ideas with one simple rule: change one thing, keep everything else the same, and watch what happens. If the result changes, you know what caused it. A cricket coach who changes only a batter's grip can see whether the grip helped. If the coach changes the bat as well, you cannot tell which change helped.

**Change one thing, keep the rest the same, and watch what happens.**

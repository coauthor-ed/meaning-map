## One Word, One List

Each word here has exactly one embedding. The nearest words to bat all come from cricket, yet a bat is also an animal that flies out of a cave at night. One list of 300 numbers has to stand for both.

**One word has one embedding, however many things it can mean.**

So when a sentence says "bat", how can a model tell which bat it means?

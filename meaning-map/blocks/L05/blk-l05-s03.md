## Compare the Two Cards

The left card shows the words nearest to girl with no sum at all, leaving out the same three words. The right card will show the top word after the sum, and our stored data keeps only that one word. Press Add the arrow, then read both cards.

**Always compare a result with what you get when you do nothing.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## The Arrow Did Not Find Woman

Woman tops both cards, so girl's own embedding was already nearest to her. You can also see boy at 0.83, which we left out and which is truly nearest to girl. What the arrow did is smaller and real: it raised woman's closeness from 0.73 to 0.81.

**The arrow did not choose woman. It moved the sum closer to her.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## Ten Capitals

One result proves little, so we tried the Paris-to-France difference on ten other capitals. The country gets closer in nine of them, and Beijing is the exception. In all ten, though, the country was already the top word with no arrow.

**The arrow often raises the partner's closeness, but the start word has already done most of the work.**

## A Picture We Chose

A drawing can make an idea look stronger than it is. Here the arrow from Tokyo ends beside Japan, and we chose it for that reason: only Tokyo and Moscow do this. Tap girl for a more usual case. That arrow ends well away from woman, because the sum uses 300 numbers and the map shows a shadow.

**A picture of an arrow proves nothing. The closeness numbers are the evidence.**

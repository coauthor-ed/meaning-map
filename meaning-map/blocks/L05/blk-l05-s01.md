## More Than Near

Near words are used in similar sentences, and boy and man are near, with a closeness of 0.62. There is also a change between them, since a man is a boy who has grown up. Could the step from one word to the other hold a meaning of its own?

**Near is one fact about two words. The step between them may be another.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## Subtract One List from the Other

If your maths marks rise from 60 to 75, the difference is 15. We find the difference between two embeddings in the same way, one number at a time, 300 times. Press Subtract to take boy's numbers from man's. You will see the first 8 results.

**The difference between two embeddings is another list of 300 numbers.**

## Draw It as an Arrow

We draw this difference as an arrow from boy to man. It is flattened like everything on the map, so the real test must use the 300 numbers. If the difference holds "grows up", adding it to another young person should reach their grown-up word. We will try girl.

**If a difference holds a pattern, it should work on a new word too.**

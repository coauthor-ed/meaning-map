## The Page Holds the Numbers

Every token the toy writes goes into the context window. The next token reads it again, like rough work in your notebook. Press Step until the toy writes its first carry.

**Written working holds the middle numbers, so each step stays small.**

## Rub Out the Carry

What if that carry were rubbed out? First predict what happens to the answer. Then press Erase carry, and press Run. The answer changes, because later steps read what was written.

**Written working can be part of the computation.**

## New Problem, Familiar Steps

The sum 4817 x 36 is in no pile. But steps like "7 x 6 = 42, carry 4" are in countless worked answers. Working turns one unseen jump into many familiar guesses.

**The problem is new, but each step in its working is familiar.**

## What Attention Does

Earlier, each word took shares from other words to make a working copy. Put simply, each position asks what it needs. Earlier positions offer what they hold. Press Move.

**Attention moves information between positions.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## Other Parts Do the Sums

Other parts of each round combine what attention brought, and change it. Press Combine. This happens again in dozens of rounds. So attention alone is not a calculator or a logic solver.

**Attention gathers. The rest of the model combines and changes.**

## What Must Be True

A constraint is something that must be true. Mina's problem has three.

The total must be 4 x 6. What is left must be the total minus 5. The answer must count pens, not packets.

**A constraint is something that must be true.**

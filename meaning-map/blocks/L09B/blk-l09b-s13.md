## Not a Built-in Solver

A solver program lists every rule and tries every option. It goes back when rules clash. It makes sure every rule holds. A language model does none of this.

**A model makes scores from learned numbers. It keeps no list of rules.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## A Picture Is Not Proof

Strong attention to 4 and 6 in a picture does not prove they caused it. To test a part, change it or switch it off. Then watch the answer.

**A test changes a part and watches what happens to the answer.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## Switch Off a Reading

Switch off the reading that lets line 2 use line 1. Now line 2 has nothing to subtract from, so the answer is lost. In our toy, that reading mattered.

**When switching off a part changes the answer, that part mattered.**

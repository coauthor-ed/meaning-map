## Job 3: Check

Asha has 30 stickers, and she has 8 more than Ben. How many stickers does Ben have?

Press Run. In the pile, "8 more than" was mostly followed by add. So the toy writes 30 + 8 = 38.

**Neat working can still be wrong.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## Put the Answer Back

Press Check. If Ben had 38, Asha would have 38 + 8 = 46, not 30.

Press Try again. Now the toy writes 30 - 8 = 22. And 22 + 8 = 30 fits.

**Putting the answer back into the story is a quick check.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## Looks Right Is Not Checked

A model can misread a link, pick the wrong operation, or slip. Its working can look right and still be wrong. A calculator, running code, or doing the sum another way can settle it.

**Job 3, check: test the answer with something outside the model.**

## Couldn't It Just Have Memorised?

A model might have memorised some facts or familiar problems. To find out, change one thing at a time.

Pick a change, and think first: should the answer change? Then tap your answer.

**To test for memory, change one thing and watch the answer.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## A Change That Breaks It

Pick Increase. The price now goes up by 15 percent.

The answer should be Rs 460. Our toy still writes Rs 340. In its pile, "What is the new price" always came after a discount.

**Failing on a small change shows that the learned way can be fragile.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## What the Test Shows

Success across many changes is evidence for patterns that can be reused. It does not prove one exact method. A real test would give a real model many problems. Ours uses a toy.

**It is not "thinks like a person", and not "only repeats text".**

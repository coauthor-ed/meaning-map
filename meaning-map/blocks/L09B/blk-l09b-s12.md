## A Real Study

Read this: "When Mary and John went to the store, John gave a drink to ___". The answer should be Mary. John is the giver, so he cannot be the one who gets the drink.

**The blank must be the name that is not repeated.**

## Three Kinds of Heads

A model has many attention parts side by side, called heads. One published study looked at a small open model, GPT-2 small. It reports 26 heads in seven groups for this task. Press Next.

**Some heads mark John. Some lower his pull. Some copy Mary.**

<!-- STEP id="s3" -->
## How They Tested It

The researchers changed parts of the model and watched the answer change. That is stronger evidence than a picture. It is one task in one small model. It does not show that every model works this way.

**Evidence comes from changing parts and watching the answer.**

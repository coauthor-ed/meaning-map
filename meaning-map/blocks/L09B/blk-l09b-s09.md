## Logic Puzzles Are Stories Too

Asha is older than Ben, and Ben is older than Chitra. Who is youngest? Press Run. The toy writes each order, joins them into Asha > Ben > Chitra, and picks the last.

**A logic puzzle also becomes a few small steps.**

<!-- STEP id="s2" -->
## One Small Word: Not

Asha, Ben and Chitra have a cat, a dog and a parrot. Ben has the parrot, and Asha does not have the dog.

Who has the dog? Here is an Example we wrote by hand:

| Example, written by us |
|---|
| Ben has the parrot. |
| So the dog is Asha's or Chitra's. |
| Asha does not have the dog. |
| So Chitra has the dog. |

**Miss the word "not", and every line after it goes wrong.**

## Rare Twists Go Wrong More

In our pile, "is older than" has many lines. "Is not older than" has only a few. Rare twists have few worked examples behind them.

**So puzzles with rare twists go wrong more often.**

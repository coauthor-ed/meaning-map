## Three Habits to Keep

The three habits you met earlier are worth keeping:

- Check what reaches you: a sure voice, a sharp picture or three matching answers prove little, so look for a source outside.
- Keep private details out of chats, and do not pass on a picture whose source you cannot trace.
- When an app helps with your work, follow your school's rules and say so.

**Check the facts, keep private details out, and say when you used an app.**
